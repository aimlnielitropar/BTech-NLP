01MODULE 01

Introduction

Computational framework · Finite State Automata · RTN / ATN
Lexicon + Algorithm + Data Structure FSA (Q,Σ,δ,q0,F) English + Indian language framework Morphological / Syntactic / Semantic / Pragmatic levels RTN & ATN Machine Translation as application
Industry takeaways
  • LLM tokenizers (BPE/WordPiece/SentencePiece in GPT, Gemini, Claude) are, mathematically, finite-state segmenters — the direct descendant of the FSA built in this module.
  • Voice assistants (Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant) chain Weighted Finite State Transducers for pronunciation and language modeling.
  • AI4Bharat / Bhashini begin Indian-language pipelines with exactly this finite-state morphological layer before any deep model runs.
Future leads for students
  • Study Weighted Finite State Transducers (WFSTs) — the production version of what you hand-built here.
  • Explore agglutinative morphology research for Indian languages via AI4Bharat's open tooling.
  • Trace how BPE/SentencePiece tokenizer training connects back to classical FSA theory.

02MODULE 02

Word Level & Syntactic Analysis

Regex · POS Tagging · CFG · Probabilistic Parsing
Regular Expressions Morphological Parsing Spelling Correction POS Tagging (rule / HMM / Viterbi) Context-Free Grammar Constituency & Probabilistic Parsing Machine-readable dictionaries
Industry takeaways
  • Search-engine indexing (Google, Bing) relies on morphological normalization exactly like the two-level parser built here, for query expansion.
  • Mobile keyboards (Gboard, SwiftKey) run noisy-channel spelling correction on-device in real time.
  • spaCy's dependency parser is the direct industrial successor to the constituency/PCFG parsers built in this module.
Future leads for students
  • Move from constituency to dependency and transition-based neural parsing (spaCy, Stanza).
  • Study CRF and BiLSTM-CRF taggers as the statistical successors to HMM/Viterbi tagging.
  • Explore treebank annotation projects for low-resource Indian languages.

03MODULE 03

Semantic Analysis

Meaning Representation · WSD · Discourse · Knowledge Reasoning
Meaning Representation Lexical Semantics Ambiguity Word Sense Disambiguation Cohesion & Reference Resolution Discourse Coherence Knowledge Representation & Reasoning
Industry takeaways
  • Voice assistants parse "turn off bedroom lights" into a semantic frame (intent/device/location) before acting on it.
  • Google Translate silently performs WSD every time it picks a target word for an ambiguous source word.
  • Google's Knowledge Graph and Wikidata are web-scale semantic networks built on the same inheritance-reasoning principle used here.
Future leads for students
  • Study how contextual embeddings (BERT) implicitly solve WSD without an explicit sense inventory.
  • Explore neural coreference resolution and the Winograd Schema Challenge as a reasoning benchmark.
  • Look into ontology engineering and knowledge-graph construction as a research/industry track.

04MODULE 04

Natural Language Generation

NLG Architecture · Machine Translation · Indian Languages
NLG Pipeline Architecture Generation Tasks & Representations Applications of NLG Problems in MT Characteristics of Indian Languages RBMT / SMT / NMT
Industry takeaways
  • Automated journalism — the Associated Press generates thousands of earnings reports and sports recaps via Wordsmith/Arria-style NLG pipelines.
  • Google Translate switched from SMT to NMT in 2016 — the exact IBM Model 1 → neural transition this module walks through.
  • Bhashini & AI4Bharat's IndicTrans2 exist specifically because generic NMT under-performs on agglutinative, SOV Indian languages.
Future leads for students
  • Explore how LLMs now perform content-determination and realization jointly, collapsing the classic NLG pipeline.
  • Study low-resource NMT techniques for Indian language pairs, and contribute to Bhashini's open datasets.
  • Learn modern MT evaluation beyond BLEU — chrF and COMET, used in WMT shared tasks.

05MODULE 05

Information Retrieval & Lexical Resources

IR Models · Evaluation · WordNet · FrameNet · Stemmers
Inverted Index Boolean / Vector Space / Probabilistic (BM25) LSI & Dense/Embedding Retrieval Precision · Recall · MAP WordNet Similarity FrameNet Stemmers & POS Taggers
Industry takeaways
  • BM25 is the default ranking function inside Elasticsearch, Solr and OpenSearch — one of the most-used scoring functions in production software.
  • Embedding-based retrieval is the backbone of every modern RAG pipeline behind chat assistants searching internal documents.
  • FrameNet-style semantic roles still power event extraction from financial news and legal documents today.
Future leads for students
  • Learn vector databases (FAISS, Pinecone, pgvector) and how they scale the dense retrieval built in this module.
  • Study hybrid search — combining BM25 with embeddings — the pattern used in most production RAG systems.
  • Explore building lexical resources for Indian languages, extending WordNet/FrameNet-style structures.
